WHERE YOU'RE LEAKING

Where your store is leaking sales
right now

Most stores lose the majority of the traffic they've already paid for. The free audit shows you exactly where — with the numbers. Here's what we almost always find.

🛒

Abandoned carts

Studies show roughly 70% of carts get abandoned — and Baymard estimates $260B of those lost orders are recoverable with better checkout and follow-up. If you're not clawing them back, that's revenue walking out the door.

  • Baymard: ~70.2% average cart abandonment across e-commerce.
  • ~$260B in lost orders is recoverable through better checkout and flows.
  • No abandoned-cart flow = you're leaving the easiest sales on the table.
⚡

Slow or weak product pages

Speed and clarity decide the sale. Google/Deloitte found every 0.1s faster mobile load lifted conversions 8.4% and average order value 9.2% — and 53% of mobile visitors leave a page that takes over 3 seconds.

  • Google/Deloitte: 0.1s faster load = +8.4% conversions, +9.2% AOV.
  • 53% of mobile visitors bounce if a page takes >3s to load.
  • Weak product pages quietly cap every campaign you run.
🎯

Wasted ad spend

Most ad accounts bleed money. WordStream found the average Google Ads account wastes about $1,127/mo — roughly a third of its budget — and 29% got zero conversions in 90 days. Without attribution, you can't tell which spend is working.

  • WordStream: avg account wastes ~$1,127/mo (~⅓ of budget).
  • 29% of accounts got zero conversions in 90 days.
  • No attribution means budget goes to the loudest channel, not the best.
📩

Weak email & SMS follow-up

Your best customers came from your list — but most stores barely use it. Studies show automated flows drive around 41% of email revenue from just ~5% of sends. Skip them and you're ignoring your cheapest sales channel.

  • Studies show flows ≈ 41% of email revenue from ~5% of sends.
  • Welcome, abandoned-cart and win-back flows recover sales on autopilot.
  • No flows = you're renting attention instead of owning it.
💸

No revenue attribution

If you can't see which channel, campaign or page actually made the sale, you're guessing — scaling losers and starving winners. Most stores fly blind here, and it's expensive.

  • No clear line from ad → click → sale means budget goes to the wrong place.
  • Winners get under-funded while losers keep running.
  • The audit maps where your revenue actually comes from.
📈

The conversion gap

The average store converts around 2.5% of visitors. Top performers convert closer to 5.5% — more than double the sales on the same traffic. That gap is the single biggest lever most owners never pull.

  • Avg store converts ~2.5% vs ~5.5% for top performers.
  • Closing the gap = more sales without spending more on traffic.
  • CRO plus continuous testing is how the top brands got there.
