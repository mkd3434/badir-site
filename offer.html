WHERE YOU'RE LEAKING

Where your store is leaking sales
right now

Most stores lose most of the traffic they've paid for. The free audit shows exactly where — here's what we usually find.

🛒

Abandoned carts

About 70% of carts get abandoned (Baymard) — and much of it is recoverable with better checkout and the follow-up flows most stores don't have.

⚡

Slow or weak product pages

Every 0.1s faster mobile load lifted conversions 8.4% (Google/Deloitte) — and 53% of visitors leave a page that takes over 3 seconds.

🎯

Wasted ad spend

The average Google Ads account wastes about a third of its budget (WordStream) — without attribution, you can't tell which spend is actually working.

📩

Weak email & SMS follow-up

Automated flows drive around 41% of email revenue from a fraction of sends — skip them and you're ignoring your cheapest sales channel.

💸

No revenue attribution

If you can't see which channel or page made the sale, you're scaling losers and starving winners. The audit maps where your revenue actually comes from.

📈

The conversion gap

The average store converts ~2.5% of visitors; top performers ~5.5% — double the sales on the same traffic. It's the biggest lever most owners never pull.
